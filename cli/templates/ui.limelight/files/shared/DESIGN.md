# Limelight · brand studio hero

The first screen of a brand studio whose pitch is being seen ("We put brands where the light is"). On a saturated yellow ground printed as a halftone, a woman with a sharp black bob stands in front of one giant white word, SPOTTED, as a pure black silhouette. (The word is chosen so it still reads with her head over its middle: the hidden letter is one of the two Ts. NOTICED and VISIBLE are the other options.) One soft warm light travels slowly over her, and wherever it falls the real colour photograph appears inside the black: an eye, the other eye, a cheek, the mouth, the fringe, and back. It must read as light, not as a hole in a mask: brightest in its middle, falling off to black, slightly warm, shaped by the face. Everything else answers the same light: the halftone dots swell and glow under it, the letters fill in solid white near it, a thin warm rim runs along the edge of her hair that faces it, and her head throws a soft shadow onto the letters on the far side. The pointer is a torch in the visitor's hand; a press fires a studio flash that shows the whole portrait for a moment.

## Images (two)

1. `portrait.webp` (3046×2206, RGBA). Prompt (square, gpt-image class model):
   "Studio portrait photograph of an East Asian woman in her early thirties with a sharp, glossy, jet black blunt bob cut at jaw length with a heavy straight fringe just above the eyebrows, wearing a black fine-knit turtleneck. Evenly lit by one large soft key light from the front, slightly above, with gentle fill, so the whole face is clearly visible. Real skin texture with visible pores and tiny imperfections, natural unretouched look, sharp catchlights in the eyes. Calm, direct, confident gaze straight into the lens, mouth closed, head perfectly upright and symmetrical, facing the camera square on. Pure seamless bright white studio backdrop, evenly lit, no shadow on the backdrop, no vignette, no gradient. Editorial studio photograph shot on a medium format digital camera with a 110mm lens at f/8, everything in sharp focus including the hair edges. Framing: head and shoulders, the head centred and large, the whole hair inside the frame with a little white margin above it, both shoulders fully inside the frame with white margin left and right, the shoulders running out of the bottom edge. No jewelry, no text, no logo."
   Pick the person by two tests: the black silhouette alone is iconic at thumbnail size, and the lit face reads as a real studio photograph.
   Cut-out: because hair and jumper are black on white, key it by brightness instead of a subject mask. Background = the white region connected to the border; alpha in a 18 px band inside the edge = `(white − darkest channel) / (white − 105)` (so fine hairs keep partial alpha), fully opaque deeper in and on anything skin-coloured; un-mix the white out of the edge pixels and clamp them dark (no halo when the flash shows the hair). Small pockets of backdrop seen through the hair by her jaw are keyed the same way. Lanczos ×1.6, unsharp (radius 1.6, 60%). Then give the picture room: 200 more rows of jumper under the frame, and 520 px more on each side where every new column is the edge column slid down by `0.5k + 0.0009k²` px, so the shoulders keep sloping out of the bottom corners instead of ending in a vertical cut. cwebp q90, alpha q100, `-exact`.
   Where things are, in units of the picture's height, x from its centre line, y from its top: eyes (∓0.086, 0.365), cheeks (±0.115, 0.465), mouth (0, 0.562), fringe (0, 0.27), hair top 0.02, chin 0.61.
2. `faces.webp` (432×144): three square avatars side by side, cropped from one generated triptych of founder headshots on flat teal, coral and cobalt backdrops.

## Layers (back to front)

1. **Still fallback** (shown until WebGL2 draws, kept if it is missing): yellow `#f6b90c`; a CSS halftone (two offset radial-gradient dot layers, 1.1 px dots of `rgba(240,112,16,.8)` on a 10 px grid); an orange-red radial glow behind the head; the word as real text, white, masked from solid at 12% to 12% opacity at 88% of its height; the photo twice: once with `filter: brightness(0)` (the silhouette), once in colour under a radial mask centred on the eyes; a soft dark-olive gradient over the bottom 16%. The WebGL pass reads the boxes of the word and the photo from these DOM elements, so the layout is defined once, in CSS.
2. **WebGL2 canvas** (one context, DPR ≤ 2, one full-screen fragment pass, about 11 texture taps a pixel, paused off screen and in a hidden tab). All maths in layout px (when the hero is shown scaled down, the canvas resolution follows the scale).
   - The light `L`, radius `R = 0.127 × photo height × light` (about 98 px at 1440×810).
   - **Idle path**: a closed Catmull-Rom loop through seven points on her (left eye, right eye, right cheek, mouth, left cheek, left temple, fringe over the right eye), one lap 17.5 s. Within each leg the parameter is `s − (0.55/2π)·sin(2πs)`, so it slows to about 45% at each point and never stops. It starts between the eyes. It always stays on the face, so in any two seconds something of her is appearing or leaving.
   - **Pointer**: `L` eases to the pointer with `1 − e^(−k·dt)`, `k` gliding between 9 (in the hand) and 2.2 (returning to the path) at rate 3, so there is no overshoot and no jump when the pointer leaves.
   - **Ground**: a 45° screen, 7 px cells (6 px on phones). Light per cell `I = .02 + .22·n² + .74·halo² + .16·halo + .46·bell(L, 2.5R) + .26·flash`, where `n` is two octaves of value noise (140 px and 43 px, the unevenness of a print) and `halo` is a Gaussian behind her head (radii .59 × .53 of the photo height, so the heat shows well past the edges of her hair while the corners stay clean yellow, its centre pulled 20% toward the light). Dot radius `cell·(.07 + .43·√I)`, then the grit of a real print: each dot × `(.74 + .5·random)` and 3% of the dots at 60% size, plus ±1.5% grain on the ground; anti-aliased by 0.75 device px. Ink runs from orange (`tone × (.98,.74,.6)` mixed 42% with `#f25712`) to red (`tone × .9` mixed 78% with `#e82912`) as `I` rises, and toward `#ff781a` under the light. Under the dots the ground is the tone, mixed up to 50% with `#fc851a` across a slightly wider halo (radii .64 × .57) and a further 42% × halo³ with `#f24d12` at its core, and 50% with `#ffd657` in a pool under the light.
   - **Word**: Archivo 800, fitted in JS so it runs edge to edge (width = 101.2% of the hero, 20.3cqi for SPOTTED), line-height .8, centred at 58% of the height. Drawn white into a canvas texture with the page's font (waits for the font, redraws on `loadingdone`). Opacity runs from 1 at the cap top to .14 at the baseline (smoothstep from 14%); near the light it fills to solid (`+ .9·bell(L, 3.1R)`, less inside her shadow); colour `#fff9e6` to pure white near the light. Her shadow: the photo's alpha from a blurred mip (about 13 px), offset away from the light by `min(.24·d, 7.5% of the photo height)`, multiplies the letters by `(.70,.47,.25)` at 40 to 80%.
   - **Person**: black by default: 3.6% of the photo (warm tinted) plus a backlit wrap at the edges (`(1 − blurred alpha)² × (.55,.27,.05) × .34`). The light adds the photo back: `E = 1.5 · e^(−(d/R)^2.4) · mix(.6, 1, n·l)`, where `n` is a normal from the photo's own blurred brightness (brighter is nearer: gradient × 3 over 0.8% of the height) and `l` points to the light held `.95R` above the picture; lit colour `photo^1.05 × (1.07,.985,.87) × min(E, 1.32)`, faded to nothing between 84.5% and 91.5% of the picture's height, so the drawn-in rows under the photograph's own frame stay black even in the flash. Rim: `1 − alpha` sampled 3.2 px toward the light (plus 35% of a 9 px soft sample), × `bell(L, 3.6R)` × 1.25, colour `#ffcc6b`.
   - A dark-olive floor over the bottom 16% (48% on phones) so the copy reads; static grain ±1%.
   - **Flash** (press anywhere on the picture, or Enter/Space on the light button): `f = min(1, t/50 ms) · e^(−4.3·(t − 50 ms))`, about 0.9 s. It adds `1.06·f` to the exposure of the whole portrait (neutral white instead of warm), swells every dot, fills the word and lifts the frame by `.10·f²`. Sound: band-passed noise 3200→700 Hz for 110 ms, a 170→55 Hz thump for 90 ms and a 12 ms tick, all under 6% gain.
   - **Parallax**: camera `c = (sin(.23t)·9, sin(.17t+1)·4.5) px` plus the eased pointer (±18, ±10 px); × .45 on phones. Ground moves −.12c, word −.42c, person −1c, copy −1.4c, cards −1.7c.
   - **Entrance** (done by 1.2 s): the light fades up, the word rises 40 px, the copy 18 px, the cards 26 px; the canvas fades in over .4 s. The silhouette and ground are there from the first frame.
3. **UI** (Archivo for everything)
   - Nav at 28 px: mark (a dark disc with a white disc inside, upper right) + "Limelight" 21/700 −.025em; links Work, Services, Studio, Contact 14.5/500; white pill "Start a project" 13.5/600, ink `#17130a`, padding 12/20.
   - Bottom left, 42 px from the bottom, 372 px wide: three 38 px avatar discs (2 px white ring, overlapping by 12 px), "Rated 4.9 by / 212 founders" 13.5/500; then the paragraph 19.5/1.3/500 −.012em, white: "We put brands where the light is. Identity, campaigns and launch films for founders done with being overlooked."
   - Bottom right, two cards 178×138, radius 18, gap 14. White card: "Brand recall" 12.5/600 with a three-dot mark, "+38%" 36/600 −.04em, "average after a relaunch" 11.5 at 62%. Dark card: a deep tint of the ground, `color-mix(in srgb, tone 27%, #170f03)` at 82% opacity (`rgba(72,54,10,.80)` on yellow), with a 14 px backdrop blur and a 1 px warm inner line: "Next opening" with a yellow dot, "March" 30/600, "2 slots left".
   - 38 and 212 count up once (0.3 to 1.5 s, ease-out cubic) and hold.

Colours: ground `#f6b90c`, ink `#17130a`, white, olive `rgba(72,54,10,.8)`, accent dot `#ffd23a`. The ground is a prop; tangerine `#fb8b1e` and rose `#f4628c` also work because the dot inks are derived from it.

## Responsive

- ≤ 900 px: nav links hidden; copy 300 px wide at 17 px; cards 150×126.
- ≤ 640 px (phone): the word moves above her (centred at 16.2% of the height, still edge to edge) with the top of her hair over its lower edge; the photo starts at 17.5% and is 66% of the height tall, so her head fills most of the width; the copy (17 px) and the two cards (side by side, flexible, 120 px tall) stack over her jumper, with the dark floor rising to 48%.

## Keyboard and reduced motion

A visually hidden button after the nav ("Move the light with the arrow keys, Enter fires the flash") becomes visible on focus: arrows move the light by 5% of the photo height, Enter or Space fires the flash, blur gives the light back to its path. Reduced motion: no entrance, no count-up, no path, no parallax: one finished frame with a slightly larger light resting between her eyes.
