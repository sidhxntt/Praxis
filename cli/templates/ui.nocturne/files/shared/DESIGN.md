# Nocturne · a streaming service whose film is already playing

Build the first screen of "Nocturne", a streaming service for slow, beautiful films. One picture fills the page and moves like a dolly shot; the interface floats over it.

**Pictures (generate, cinematic photographs, dusk light, haze, fine grain, no text).** Three films, each one strong shape and small figures. Film 1, "The Salt Road": a huge mirrored disc standing on a salt flat at blue hour, a parked car with its lamps on far away; and, as a separate cut-out with a transparent ground, two women seen from behind in the right foreground. Film 2, "Low Tide, Late Summer": a pier running to a round glass pavilion over a flat silver sea at dawn. Film 3, "A House with No North": a house-sized stone cube balanced in a wheat field under a pink sky. Also a small crop of each for the trailer thumbnail.

**Layout.** Top: logo mark + "NOCTURNE" in spaced caps; nav centred (Films · Series · Collections · Directors · Journal, the first marked with a dot); a glass "Search" pill and a round profile key right. Bottom left: a rounded trailer thumbnail with a play ring and the label TRAILER; a meta row with small icons (8.4/10 · 118 min · March 2026); the title in a large light grotesk (about 4.4% of the width); a thin progress line with "01 / 03"; one line of story ("Two sisters drive a lighthouse lamp across a dried sea."); a white pill "Play film" with a play dot and an outlined pill "More about it". Bottom right: outlined pills "Previous" and "Next".

**Type and colour.** One light grotesk (Outfit, Urbanist or similar; 300 for the title, 400 for the rest). White text; glass keys are white at 10% with a hairline border and a blur. Night blue #0b101c behind the picture.

**Motion.** From the first frame the picture pushes in slowly while the foreground cut-out comes nearer a little faster. Haze drifts across the horizon, a few birds cross, dust hangs in the light and the grain keeps moving. The progress line fills in about 4.5 seconds; then the page cuts to the next film: the new picture wipes in behind a soft light leak, the title's letters roll up and the new ones roll in, the rating and minutes count to their new values, the thumbnail swaps. Previous, Next and the arrow keys do the same by hand. The pointer parts the layers; pointing at the thumbnail plays a slow pan inside it. "Play film" draws cinema bars in for a moment. With reduced motion, show film 1 still.

**Phone.** The picture fills the screen, the title block sits at the bottom, Previous and Next become two round keys.
