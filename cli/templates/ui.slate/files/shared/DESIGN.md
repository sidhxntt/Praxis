# Slate · an architecture studio showing one house

Build the first screen of "Nordhus architects" presenting the "SLATE house". The house's name is set very large across the sky BEHIND the house, so the roof cuts into the letters, and a script word lies in front.

**Pictures (generate, real photographs, no text).** (1) Sky plate: heavy grey cloud tearing on a dark mountain, with one break of pale light. (2) A separate wide strip of cloud that can slide. (3) The house and its ground as a cut-out on a transparent ground: a long, low house of dark timber and slate on black rock above a fjord, tufts of yellow grass, one corner of glass lit warm from inside. The roofline must be a clean edge.

**Layers, back to front.** Sky, sliding cloud, the giant word, the house cut-out, the script word, mist and a thin rain, the interface.

**Words.** Pills top left: Home (filled white) · Studio · Houses · Journal · Fees. Logo centred: a 3×3 grid of squares with one missing + "NORDHUS architects". Right: "Contact" and "Start a house" with a long arrow. Giant word "SLATE" in a wide grotesk, cream #efe6d8, about 27% of the width high. Script word "house" (Pinyon Script or an italic display serif) over its lower right. Notes: "2026 / A house that keeps the weather outside" and three right-aligned lines "— Low", "— Sheltered", "— Quiet". A glass info card: Studio: Nordhus architects · Architects: Ingrid Vaa, Tomás Reyes · Place: Lofoten, Norway · Area: 164 m² · Year: 2024 · Type: Private house. Three hotspot dots on the house (the roof, the glass corner, the stilts), each with one line of its own. Two short paragraphs bottom right. Three small squares bottom left with an arrow, an envelope and a grid.

**Motion.** The camera never stands still: it drifts sideways on a slow loop, the sky and the house move by different amounts, and the name slides behind the roof. Cloud crosses the sky, mist crosses the mountain, a band of light travels over the rock and the glass corner glows as it passes, a thin rain falls in front. The three hotspots pulse in turn; the glass card springs from one to the next, redraws its hairline and rewrites itself. The pointer adds to the parallax; hovering or tabbing to a hotspot holds its card. With reduced motion, one still frame with the first card.

**Phone.** The house fills the lower two thirds, the word runs behind it at a size that fits, the card becomes a sheet at the bottom.
